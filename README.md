## Overview

This repository contains **5 novel patent applications** for mathematical learning devices that combine **wearable technology, AI, tactile feedback, and solar power** to revolutionize how mathematics is taught and learned.

Each patent addresses a unique gap in current educational technology, targeting children, students, and lifelong learners with accessibility-first design.

## Innovation Highlights

- **Tactile mathematics** - Feel equations through vibration patterns on your arm
- **AR math overlay** - See mathematical relationships in your physical environment
- **Solar-powered AI tutor** - Works offline in rural schools without electricity
- **3D math fabrication** - Create physical models of parametric surfaces
- **Neurofeedback for numeracy** - Enhance numerical cognition through gentle stimulation
